About me

I'm an AI researcher focused on large language models, NLP and multimodal learning. I build systems that hold up across languages, domains and low-resource settings, and I care most about data scarcity, class imbalance and cross-lingual transfer. The applications I work on include social media analysis, healthcare and public safety.

My MS in Computer Science is from FAST-NUCES, Pakistan's top-ranked university for Computer Science. My thesis was on speech emotion recognition for Urdu, using transfer learning, deep neural networks and multimodal representations. That grew into work on cross-lingual sentiment analysis, threat detection and multimodal fake-news detection with transformers and LLMs. I've also spent 5+ years as a Lecturer in AI and Computer Science at Bahria University and Capital University of Science and Technology.
